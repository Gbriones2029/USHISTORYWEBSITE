The Life & Endeavors of Mr. Franklin

Benjamin Franklin was born in Boston in 1706 and rose to become one of the most distinguished polymaths of the Western world. As a printer, author, diplomat, and philosopher, his contributions laid the cornerstone for American democracy and scientific discovery.

In public service, Mr. Franklin created the lightning rod, helping prevent fire all around the colonies. His work in electricity and his experiments with the kite and key were groundbreaking.

The Invention of the Lightning Rod

  1. Studied Static Electricity: Began extensive experiments with electrical equipment and batteries to understand how electrical charge behaves .
  2. Proposed the Concept: Suggested that mounting Rods could provide a path for electrical discharge safely into the ground.
  3. Published Theories: Published this theory in his writings/books.
  4. Kite Experiment: Flew a kite through a thunderstorm and proved that storm clouds carry electrical energy
  5. Build the First Lightning Rod: Installed a Rod on his House and tested how it safely conducted electricity to the ground.

The Lightning Rod

In the 1700s, lightning was dangerous because wood easily caught fire. This could be a major issue for any lumber yards, forests, and structures that used any wood at all His invention safely guided atmospheric electricity into the ground through the use of brass wires and iron rods.

Most Common Use

House and barns hold cash crops were some of the most common uses for the lightning rod. It would help redirect the lightning to avoid damaging these structures along with the cash crops. This would prove to be very important to the agricultural community.

Chronicles of a Founder

  • 1706: Born in Boston, Massachusetts on January 17.
  • 1729: Becomes owner and publisher of The Pennsylvania Gazette.
  • 1752: Conducts the famous electrical kite experiment.
  • 1776: Helps draft the Declaration of Independence.
  • 1790: Passes away on April 17 in Philadelphia at age 84.

LongTerm Impact

  • New Words:Words such as batteries, positive and negative, were coined by Benjamin Franklin. to help spark new ideas.
  • Lightning Rod: Helped prevent fire/ improved fire safety and awareness through the use of lightning rods (Metal rods used to divert lightning away from flamable items)
  • Intellectual Prowess: His intelegence was known for its depth and wisdom. This would later prove to be a defining characteristic of his legacy and empower the American people to lead on the advancements he had pioneered.

Facts About Benjamin Franklin

  • Patented?: He distributed the knowledge of how to build it and did not patent the product so people can use it and save their lives.
  • Myth of the Kite: Franklin's kite never got hit by lightning; rather, it was charged by an ambient electrical charge from the storm.
  • The Protector of Homes: His lightning rod is still used today to help protect millions of homes and buildings from lightning.
  • Original?: Not the first to suggest that idea, nor even the first to perform the experiment.
  • The Theory of Lightning: He hypothesized that lightning was simply a bigger and larger form of static electricity.

Vocabulary Words

  • Cash Crops: Agricultural products grown for sale and profit rather than for subsistence. Having lightning strikes hit cash crop barns, it can reduce the amount of money being made. His invention of the lightning rod helped protect these valuable assets.
  • Mercantilism : An economic theory that emphasizes the importance of exports and the accumulation of precious metals like gold and silver. Now, he shows the American intelligence by making his own ideas and inventions.
  • Indentured Servants: Urban Tradesmen had to create and install the lightning rods that he had invented. This required significant labor and expertise. Thus opening up new opportunities for skilled craftsmen.
